Maat Studio

Reference photos built from what you actually drew — nothing added, nothing invented.

What we build

Artists can't always afford live models, a studio, or professional lighting for reference photos. Our tool takes an artist's own drawing — or a painting in progress — and turns it into a realistic reference photo of exactly what was drawn. The artist refines the next draft, and the tool renders again.

It is a collaboration tool, not an image generator. It never invents content from a text prompt, and it never adds anything the artist didn't put on the page.

The core principle

Named for Ma'at, the ancient principle of truth and alignment

In Egyptian thought, to build "in Ma'at" meant working with absolute fidelity between intention and outcome. That's the one rule our rendering pipeline is built around: the output always matches what the artist drew. No unprompted objects, no invented limbs, no content the artist didn't put there.

How it works

  1. Upload a drawing A sketch, line drawing, or a painting still in progress — the artist's own work, not a photograph.
  2. Answer a few quick questions The tool asks about ambiguous parts of the drawing so nothing gets guessed wrong.
  3. Get a reference photo back A realistic, greyscale rendering of precisely what was drawn, ready to paint or draw from.
  4. Revise and repeat Adjust the drawing, ask for a different light, or request a new version, and keep working from there.

Safety and integrity

Every render, on every engine, passes through the same checks before anything reaches the artist.

  • Server-side classification. Every uploaded drawing is checked before rendering; the tool refuses to process an actual photograph.
  • Age and content check on every render. A dedicated check runs before and after generation, on every engine, with no override for a positive match on apparent nudity plus apparent minor age.
  • Fails closed. If a safety check can't complete, the render is blocked rather than allowed through.
  • Consent-gated mature content. Mature renders require the artist to affirmatively attest to being an adult before anything is generated.
  • No real-person likeness in mature renders. An artist's own reference photos of real people cannot be attached to a render that reads as mature content.
