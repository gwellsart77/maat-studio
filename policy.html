Sexual Content and Minor Safety Policy

How Maat Studio protects minors and handles adult figure work.

What Maat Studio is

Maat Studio is a reference tool for visual artists. An artist uploads their own drawing, and the app renders a realistic greyscale reference photograph of exactly what they drew. It is not a text-to-image generator: every image starts from the artist's own drawing. Photographs cannot be uploaded to be rendered.

Maat Studio is currently in private development and is not yet open to the public. This policy applies now and will be reviewed before public launch.

1. Zero tolerance for minors

Maat Studio never creates nude or sexual imagery of anyone who is, or appears to be, under 18. There are no exceptions, and no setting, plan or user request changes this.

2. Adult figure work

Figure drawing of adults, including nudity, is a long-standing part of fine art practice, and Maat Studio supports it. Before any mature render, the user must confirm that they are 18 or older, that every person depicted is an adult, and that they have the right to make the work.

3. How we enforce this

  • Every upload is checked before rendering. Our server classifies each image itself before it is sent for rendering: whether it is a photograph (refused), whether it contains nudity, and whether any figure appears to be under 18. Nudity together with an apparent minor is refused outright. This check runs on our server and cannot be switched off from the user's browser.
  • Every result is checked before the user sees it. Each image the app returns is reviewed again by a separate automated check. Nudity together with an apparent minor is blocked.
  • Checks fail closed. If either check cannot complete, nothing is rendered or returned.
  • No real people in mature work. Photographs cannot be turned into renders. A user's own reference photos of real people cannot be used with mature content, and a render that comes back nude while such photos were attached is refused.
  • No open prompts. Users cannot type a free description of an image to be generated. They can add short notes and answers about what they drew, which are placed into a fixed set of instructions around the uploaded drawing.
  • Provider filters stay in place. The image models we use apply their own content filters in addition to ours.

4. Known limits

Age and nudity detection are automated judgments and can be wrong. Where a check is uncertain, we design it to refuse rather than allow.

5. Reporting

If we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material connected to Maat Studio, we will refuse it, preserve the relevant information, report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline, and cooperate with law enforcement. Anyone who tries to use Maat Studio to create sexual imagery of a minor will be refused service.

Concerns can be reported to gwellsart@gmail.com.
